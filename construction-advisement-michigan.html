Construction Advisement in Mid‑Michigan

Engineers design it. We help you build it. Great Lakes SiteLogic provides real-world advisement on construction process and materials — the practical judgment that only comes from years with boots in the dirt, applied to your project before small decisions become expensive problems.

Beyond the engineering desk

Plans and specs tell you what to build. They don't always tell you the best way to build it — the sequencing that saves a week, the material choice that performs better in Michigan soils and weather, or the field adjustment that keeps the job moving when conditions change. That's where we come in.

What we advise on

  • Construction process and sequencing for earthwork and site work
  • Material selection: what performs, what lasts, what's worth the cost
  • Reading plans with a builder's eye — catching issues before they cost money
  • Field problem-solving when conditions don't match the design
  • Practical value engineering that doesn't compromise the job

Judgment you can't download

Our advisement comes from 36 combined years across takeoff, civil consulting, grade-control modeling, machine control, and survey — 16 of them firsthand in the field. We've seen what works, what fails, and what the plans missed. That experience is what you're buying.

Frequently asked questions

Is this engineering services?

No. We don't stamp drawings or replace your engineer. We provide practical construction advisement — process, materials, and field judgment — that complements the engineering.

When should we bring you in?

The earlier the better. Advisement during planning and takeoff prevents the most expensive problems, but we can also help mid-project when something isn't working.

Do you come to the jobsite?

Yes. Some advisement happens over plans and a phone call; the valuable kind usually happens standing on the dirt looking at the actual conditions.
