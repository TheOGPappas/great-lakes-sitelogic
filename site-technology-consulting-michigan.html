Site Technology Consulting in Mid‑Michigan

Construction technology only pays off when it fits how your crew actually works. Great Lakes SiteLogic helps contractors choose, set up, and get value from site technology and software — from field mapping apps to grade control workflows — without the enterprise-software runaround.

Technology that fits the job

We start with what you're trying to accomplish, not with a product catalog. Whether it's getting your crew off paper plans, setting up a field data workflow, or making your office and field talk to each other, we recommend practical setups sized for your company — from 1-to-10-person shops to large operations.

What we consult on

  • Field mapping and GIS workflows for construction crews
  • Grade control and machine control technology setup
  • Software selection: what to buy, what to skip, and what free tools cover
  • Connecting office design data (CAD, Civil 3D) to field devices
  • Practical rollout: getting crews trained and actually using the tech

Built from the dirt up

Our advice comes from 16 years of boots-in-the-dirt experience, not a sales deck. We've run the equipment, managed the work, and built the models — so we know which technology survives contact with a real jobsite and which doesn't.

Frequently asked questions

Do you sell software or hardware?

No. We don't sell software licenses or hardware. That means our recommendations aren't tied to a commission — we tell you what actually works for your situation.

Can you help us get started with FieldSight?

Yes. We built FieldSight, so we can get your crew set up, import your project data, and train your team on the workflows that matter for your jobs.

We already have software nobody uses. Can you fix that?

Usually, yes. Most "failed" software rollouts are training and workflow problems, not software problems. We figure out where it broke down and get the crew using it.
