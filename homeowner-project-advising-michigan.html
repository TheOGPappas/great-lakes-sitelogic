Homeowner Project Advising in Mid‑Michigan

Big project on your property and not sure where to start? Great Lakes SiteLogic advises homeowners on site work — grading, drainage, driveways, and land improvements — so you hire the right contractor, pay a fair price, and get work that actually holds up.

Someone in your corner

Contractors know their business. You should have someone who knows yours. We help homeowners understand what their project really needs, what it should cost, and how to tell good work from a shortcut — before the contract is signed and before the first load of dirt moves.

What we advise on

  • Grading and drainage problems around your home
  • Driveway, parking area, and access improvements
  • Evaluating contractor bids: what's fair, what's missing, what's a red flag
  • Understanding what the work involves so you can ask the right questions
  • Oversight input while work is underway

Straight answers, no jargon

We explain your project in plain language — what's wrong, what fixes it, what it should cost, and what to watch for. No sales pitch, no contractor referral kickbacks. Just honest guidance from people who've done this work for years.

Frequently asked questions

Do you do the construction work yourselves?

We advise; we don't swing the hammer on homeowner jobs. That keeps our guidance independent — we're not steering you toward our own crew.

Can you look at my yard's drainage problem?

Yes. Drainage and grading issues are the most common homeowner concerns we advise on. We can assess what's happening and lay out your options.

How do I know if a bid is fair?

Bring us the bids. We'll walk through what's included, what's missing, and whether the price lines up with the work described.
