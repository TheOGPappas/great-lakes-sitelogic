Pre-Construction Consulting in Mid‑Michigan

The cheapest problems to solve are the ones you catch before construction starts. Great Lakes SiteLogic helps contractors and developers work through initial development ideas — feasibility, budgeting, and planning — while changes still cost nothing.

Think it through before you build it

Got a development idea, a parcel you're eyeing, or a project in the early stages? We help you pressure-test it: what the site will really take to develop, realistic earthwork quantities and costs, the sequencing that makes sense, and the risks hiding in the early assumptions.

What we consult on

  • Initial development idea review and feasibility input
  • Early earthwork quantity estimates and cost opinions
  • Site evaluation: grading challenges, drainage, and access
  • Pre-construction planning and sequencing
  • Budget development grounded in real production rates
  • Risk identification before you're committed

Ideas meet reality

Early-stage advice is only valuable if it's honest. We tell you what the site is really going to take — not what you want to hear. Our input comes from 36 combined years of takeoff, modeling, machine control, and field work, so our early numbers hold up when the job starts.

Frequently asked questions

We're just kicking around an idea. Is it too early to talk?

No — that's the best time. Early input is cheap and prevents the expensive surprises that show up after you're committed.

Can you help us decide whether to pursue a project?

Yes. We give you straight input on feasibility, likely costs, and risks so you can make the go/no-go call with real information.

Do you work with developers or just contractors?

Both. Developers get feasibility and budgeting input; contractors get pre-construction planning that sets the job up to win.
