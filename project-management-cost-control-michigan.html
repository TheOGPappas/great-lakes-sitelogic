Project Management and Cost Control in Mid‑Michigan

Jobs make money when someone watches the schedule, the costs, and the crew — all at once. Great Lakes SiteLogic helps contractors manage projects tighter: practical project advisement, real cost control, and team development that keeps production moving.

Management from people who've run work

We don't manage from a spreadsheet. Our project advisement comes from years of running equipment, managing crews, and living with the consequences of schedule and budget decisions. We help you set up the controls that catch problems early — while they're still cheap to fix.

What we help with

  • Project planning and schedule advisement for site and civil work
  • Cost tracking and cost control systems that crews will actually use
  • Production analysis: where the hours are going and why
  • Crew leadership and team development for foremen and operators
  • Owner and subcontractor coordination support

Cost control that works in the field

Cost control fails when it's paperwork nobody fills out. We set up simple, field-friendly tracking — daily production, quantities, and costs — so you know where every job stands before the month-end surprise. And we develop your team to own it, not just report it.

Frequently asked questions

Do you take over as the project manager?

We can advise your PM team or provide hands-on project management support, depending on what the job needs. Either way, the goal is a stronger operation, not a dependency.

We're losing money and don't know where. Can you help?

Yes. We dig into production, quantities, and costs to find where the bleed is — then set up controls so it doesn't happen on the next job.

What does team development look like?

Practical leadership growth for foremen and crew leads: running work efficiently, communicating with the office, tracking production, and developing the operators under them.
