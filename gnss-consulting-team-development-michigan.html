GNSS Consulting and Team Development in Mid‑Michigan

Good satellite positioning is a crew skill, not just a hardware purchase. Great Lakes SiteLogic helps contractors get real value from GNSS — choosing the right receivers, setting up reliable workflows, and developing the team members who run them every day.

Receivers, corrections, and real accuracy

We help you navigate the GNSS landscape: which receivers fit your work, how NTRIP corrections and base stations actually perform in Michigan conditions, and what accuracy you can honestly expect. No inflated spec-sheet promises — just what works on real jobsites.

What we consult on

  • GNSS receiver selection for your work type and budget
  • Base station, NTRIP, and correction setup
  • Field workflows: control checks, staking, and as-builts
  • Troubleshooting accuracy problems and bad shots
  • Integrating GNSS data with grade control and CAD workflows

Develop your team, not just your toolbox

Equipment without trained operators is an expensive paperweight. We develop your crew's GNSS skills hands-on — on your gear, on your jobs. New operators learn setup, checking into control, staking, and recognizing when a number doesn't look right. Experienced hands get sharper on corrections, coordinate systems, and troubleshooting.

Frequently asked questions

Do you sell GNSS receivers?

No. We don't sell or rent receivers. Our advice on hardware is independent — we recommend what fits your work, not what pays us.

Can you train our whole crew?

Yes. Training is built around your equipment and your typical jobs, from new operators to experienced crew leads who want to go deeper.

We have accuracy problems we can't explain. Can you help?

That's exactly what we do. We work through the setup — corrections, coordinate systems, control, field technique — until the numbers make sense.
