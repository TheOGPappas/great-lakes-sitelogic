## How a takeoff works with us

1. **Send the plan set.** PDF plans are fine. CAD files and geotechnical reports help if you have them.
2. **Confirm the scope.** We confirm what you need counted, your bid due date, and any assumptions, such as topsoil depth or pavement section.
3. **We do the takeoff.** Surfaces and quantities are built from the plans and checked against each other.
4. **You get organized numbers.** Quantities come itemized and labeled, with notes on assumptions and plan issues we found.

## What you receive

- Topsoil stripping and respread quantities

## Why outsource takeoffs?

Bid season stacks up fast. Outsourcing takeoffs lets your estimator focus on pricing, subs, and strategy instead of counting. It also gives you a second set of eyes on the plans, which catches problems before they turn into change orders or losses.

## Frequently asked questions

### Can you take off from PDF plans only?
Yes. Most takeoffs start from PDF plan sets. CAD files can speed up the work and improve accuracy on complex grading.

### Can the takeoff surfaces be used for machine control later?
Often, yes. If you win the job, the surfaces built for the takeoff can be the starting point for your grade-control model, which saves time at mobilization.

### How do you account for shrink and swell?
We report bank volumes and note the assumptions used. Shrink and swell factors can be applied to match your experience with local soils.
