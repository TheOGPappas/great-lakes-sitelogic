# Civil 3D Grade-Control Modeling in Michigan

Most Civil 3D models are built for plan production, not for machines. The difference shows up the moment your grade control system tries to read the surface. Great Lakes SiteLogic builds Civil 3D models specifically for grade control: surfaces your machines can trust, corridors that tie out, and data structured the way field crews need it.

## Modeled for the machine, not just the plan set

A pretty corridor profile doesn't help a dozer operator. We build finished-grade surfaces, subgrade surfaces, and the supporting linework with machine control in mind from the start. That means proper targeting, clean daylighting, consistent assembly logic, and surfaces that don't have the spikes and gaps that come from models built for plotting.

## What you get

- Finished-grade and subgrade surface models built for grade control export
- Corridor modeling with correct assemblies, targets, and frequency settings
- Model review and cleanup: take an existing design model and make it field-usable
- Revisions turned around fast when the engineer issues a change
