# Design Consulting in Mid-Michigan

Good designs get built faster, cheaper, and with fewer headaches. Great Lakes SiteLogic provides design consulting grounded in construction reality — civil design guidance from people who've spent years building what the plans describe, not just drawing it.

## Designed to be built

Too many designs look clean on paper and fight you in the field. We consult on the practical side of civil design: grading that drains, layouts a crew can actually stake, details that don't require a miracle to construct. Our input comes from {{yrs:2010}} years of boots-in-the-dirt experience plus deep Civil 3D modeling expertise.

## What we consult on

- Practical review of civil site designs before they go to bid
- Grading, drainage, and earthwork design guidance
- 3D modeling support: surfaces, corridors, and quantities in Civil 3D
- Design-for-constructability: details crews can actually build
- Coordination between design intent and field execution

## From model to machine

We don't just review designs — we build the 3D models that drive machine control and layout. When we consult on a design, we're thinking about how it becomes a surface model, how the dozer reads it, and how the crew stakes it. That end-to-end view catches problems early.

## Frequently asked questions

### Do you stamp engineering drawings?
No. We provide design consulting and 3D modeling support. Stamped engineering comes from your licensed engineer — we make their designs more buildable.

### Can you review a design we're about to bid?
Yes. A pre-bid design review often pays for itself many times over by catching quantities issues, constructability problems, or missed details before you're committed.

### Do you work with our engineer?
Absolutely. We coordinate with your design team to resolve field concerns while respecting the engineering intent.
