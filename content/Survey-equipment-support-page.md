# Survey Equipment Support in Mid-Michigan

Survey gear is only useful when it's working and your crew knows how to run it. A base station that won't connect or a rover giving bad shots can stall a whole job. Great Lakes SiteLogic supports contractors' survey equipment so small problems don't become lost days.

## Keep your crew running

We help with the practical side of survey equipment: getting GPS and total station setups working, troubleshooting when shots don't tie, and getting new crew members up to speed on the gear. You don't need a survey department, you need someone who answers when the equipment acts up.

## What we support

- GPS and total station setup and troubleshooting
- Base station and rover configuration issues
- Help getting new operators trained on survey equipment
- Workflow support connecting survey data to your grade control models

## Local, responsive help

We're family-owned and based in Mid-Michigan, serving contractors in Saginaw, Bay City, and Midland directly, with remote troubleshooting anywhere in Michigan. When your gear isn't cooperating on a Tuesday morning, you talk to us, not a support portal.

## Don't lose a day to a setup problem

Most survey equipment issues are fixable fast by someone who's seen them before. That's us.
