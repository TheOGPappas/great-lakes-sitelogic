# Field Mapping & GIS Services in Michigan

Jobsites run on information, and half the time it's stuck in someone's head or on a crumpled plan in the truck. Great Lakes SiteLogic builds field mapping and GIS workflows that put real site data in your crew's hands: where things are, what was done, and what changed.

## Maps that work on the jobsite

We set up field mapping systems your crew will actually use: site feature collection, as-built capture, progress tracking, and quantity verification, all tied to real coordinates. Data flows from the field back to the office cleanly, so the trailer and the cab are looking at the same thing.

## What we do

- Field data collection setup and workflows for construction sites
- GIS mapping of site features, utilities, and as-built conditions
- Progress and quantity tracking tied to real locations
- Maps and data formatted for the people who have to use them in the field

## Practical, not academic

This isn't enterprise GIS consulting with a six-month timeline. We're a Mid-Michigan, family-owned shop that works with contractors in Saginaw, Bay City, and Midland, and we build mapping setups sized for real construction operations. Remote support available anywhere in Michigan.


## Common jobsite uses

- Locating and recording existing utilities, structures, and site features
- As-built capture of installed pipe, structures, and finished grades
- Tracking daily progress and installed quantities by location
- Marking punch-list items, damage, and problem areas with photos and coordinates
- Sharing site maps with foremen, superintendents, and owners

## How we set it up

1. **Understand the workflow.** What the crew needs to collect, who uses the data, and what the office needs back.
2. **Set up coordinates and layers.** Project coordinate system, control, design references, and feature types.
3. **Get the crew running.** Simple field collection on the devices you already have, with short training.
4. **Deliver usable outputs.** Maps, as-built files, and exports in formats like CSV, KML, GeoJSON, and DXF.

## Frequently asked questions

### Do we need expensive GIS software?
No. Most crews can collect and view data in a browser on the phones and tablets they already carry. We set things up for the size of your operation.

### How accurate is the location data?
Accuracy depends on the device. Phones give a general location, while connected GNSS receivers can deliver survey-grade positions. We'll help you match the equipment to the job.

### Can field data come back into CAD?
Yes. Collected points and features can be exported to CSV, DXF, KML, and GeoJSON for use in CAD, GIS, and reporting.
