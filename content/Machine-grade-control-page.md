# Machine Grade Control Services in Mid-Michigan

Machine grade control only saves you money when the model is right. A bad surface in the system means your dozers and graders are cutting to the wrong numbers, and you find out about it when the dirt's already moved. Great Lakes SiteLogic builds and supports grade control models that hold up in the field.

## Built for the way you actually work

We work with the file formats your machines run on and the grade control systems your operators already know. Models come field-ready: clean surfaces, correct offsets, and linework that makes sense to the guy in the cab. When something doesn't look right at 6 AM on a jobsite in Bay City, you get a real person who speaks dirt, not a ticket queue.

- Grade control model building and surface prep for dozers, graders, and excavators
- File conversion and troubleshooting when models won't load or don't tie out
- Support for setting up new machine control systems and getting crews up to speed

## How it works

1. **Send the files.** Plans, CAD/DXF, LandXML, existing surfaces, and control points, whatever you have.
2. **We review the design.** We check for missing data, bad elevations, and conflicts before anything is built.
3. **We build the model.** Surfaces, linework, and alignments are prepared for your specific machines and grade control system.
4. **Field check and handoff.** Files are delivered ready to load, with notes on control, localization, and anything the crew should know. We can also meet your crew on site to load files and check into control.
5. **Revisions as the job moves.** When the engineer issues a change, we update the model and track which version is current.

## What we deliver

- Finished-grade, subgrade, and stripping surfaces
- Alignments and linework for curbs, pads, ditches, and utilities
- Files formatted for the grade control system your machines already run
- A short handoff summary: control used, revision date, and known plan issues
